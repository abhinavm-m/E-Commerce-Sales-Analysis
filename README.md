# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes e-commerce sales performance across regions, products, countries, and payment channels. It helps business users see what drives revenue and profit and where to focus next.

## 🎯 Objective
To turn raw e-commerce transaction data into clear, actionable insights on sales, profit, product performance, regional contribution, and customer payment behavior.

## 📌 Key Metrics (KPIs)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 2.79M |
| Total Profit | 615.39K |
| Total Quantity Sold | 7K |
| Most Preferred Payment Method | Bank Transfer |

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals
- **Region-Wise Sales** (donut chart): sales split across North America, South America, Asia Pacific, and Europe.
- **Product-Wise Profit** (bar chart): the most profitable products, ranked.
- **Product-Wise Quantity** (column chart): units sold per product.
- **Count of Pay Mode** (pie chart): orders by Phone Order, In-Store, Marketplace, and Online.
- **Interactive slicers**: filter everything by Product, Region, and Country.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **North America** is the top region at about 27.7% of total sales, but all four regions contribute between 23% and 28%, so sales are well balanced.
- **Vacuum Cleaner** and **Cookware Set** are the most profitable products (about 37K and 35K), followed by Coffee Maker and Blender (about 31K each).
- The best-selling products by quantity (Vacuum Cleaner, Coffee Maker, Cookware Set) are also among the top profit generators.
- **Phone Orders** (27.75%) and **In-Store** purchases (26.67%) are the most common order channels, ahead of Marketplace and Online.
- Orders are spread across 16 countries, including the USA, UK, India, Germany, Japan, and Brazil.
